My Story

Who Am I?

A Woman on a Mission

I am LLady Peace — a woman who has walked through darkness and emerged with a light that refuses to be dimmed. Through my own battles with depression, anxiety, grief, and heartbreak, I discovered that my pain had a purpose: to help other women find their way back to themselves.

Everything I do is centered around one message: Never Give Up. Those three words are more than a motto — they are how I live my life. I believe God has a unique plan for every person, and even when the road isn't easy, giving up means never discovering the incredible future waiting on the other side.

Through my books, journals, the Who Am I? app, Sisterhood Circle community, speaking engagements, and notary services, my hope is that someone will find the courage to keep going, believe again, and realize that their story isn't over.

Because no matter what you've been through... Your past does not define you. Your pain does not disqualify you. And with God, your best chapters are still being written.
